## Why this exists

Nothing Left is licensed under **AGPL-3.0** (see [LICENSE](./LICENSE)).

AGPL alone does not let the project maintainer relicense contributed code —
for example, to offer a commercial edition to a customer whose legal team
refuses AGPL. Without a CLA, doing so would require tracing and obtaining
permission from every past contributor, which in practice is impossible.

This agreement keeps that option open. It does **not** take your copyright
away: you keep full ownership of everything you write, and you may use your
own contribution anywhere else, for any purpose. You are granting a licence,
not assigning ownership.

Your contribution also remains available to everyone under AGPL-3.0. Nothing
here can be used to remove code from the open-source project.

The one exception is `packages/mcp-stdio`, the `@nothingleft/mcp` client that people
install next to their agent. It is MIT-licensed (see its [LICENSE](./packages/mcp-stdio/LICENSE)),
so a contribution to that directory is available to everyone under MIT.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA assistant will ask you to confirm, once, that
you have read and agree to this document. Your confirmation is recorded
against your GitHub account and covers all of your future contributions.

For a first contribution before any automation is in place, add this line to
your pull request description:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.
```
